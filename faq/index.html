Indian tax laws provide clear mechanisms for overseas Indian investors:
  • Zero Tax on Purchase: No special tax is levied at the time of property purchase other than standard stamp duty and registration fees applicable to all Indian citizens.
  • Rental Repatriation: Rental earnings deposited into NRO accounts are 100% repatriable up to USD $1 Million per financial year under RBI guidelines upon submission of Form 15CA & 15CB.
  • Double Taxation Relief (DTAA): Taxes paid in India can be claimed as a tax credit in countries holding DTAA treaties with India (e.g., USA, UK, UAE, Canada, Singapore, Australia).
  • Lower TDS Certificates: Our legal advisory desk assists NRIs in obtaining Section 197 certificates for reduced withholding tax upon eventual resale.
